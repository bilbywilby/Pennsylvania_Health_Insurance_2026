# How to Gather Records and Fill Out Dispute Templates Step by Step

Before sending any dispute letter, financial assistance application, or regulatory complaint, gather your key papers and fill out only facts you can verify from your records.

## Step 1: Gather Your Core Records First

- **Itemized bill from the provider:** Ask for an itemized statement showing each service line, date of service, code, unit count, and billed dollar amount—not just a summary balance.
- **Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your insurer:** Match each billed charge to how your health plan processed it (allowed amount, plan payment, deductible, copay, coinsurance, or denial reason).
- **Letters, portals, and call notes:** Save every bill, collection notice, financial assistance form, and a simple log of dates, phone reference numbers, and who you spoke with.
- **Identity and account details:** Have your policy/member ID, claim number, account number, and service dates ready (and protect sensitive personal IDs when sharing or storing copies).

## Step 2: Pick the Right Template for Your Situation

- **Surprise out-of-network or emergency/ancillary bill:** See [[Template-Act-252-NSA-Rescission-Demand]] and [[Surprise-Bill-Playbook]].
- **Hospital financial hardship or charity care:** See [[Template-Financial-Assistance-IRS-501-r-Request]] and [[Hospital-Financial-Assistance-Guide]].
- **Collection agency demand or disputed/old balance:** See [[Template-Debt-Validation-Statutory-Dispute-Notice]] and [[Medical-Debt-Defense-Playbook]].
- **Provider hold or billing escalation:** See [[Template-Provider-Escalation-Administrative-Hold-Demand]].
- **Insurer or plan complaint:** See [[Template-PID-Complaint-Narrative-Fully-Insured]] or [[Template-CMS-Complaint-Narrative-Self-Funded-ERISA]].
- **Overview & rules first:** Review [[Template-Library]] and [[Before-You-Fill-Out-a-Template]].

## Step 3: Fill Out the Template Safely

1. **Replace every bracketed placeholder (`[like this]`)** with exact dates, dollar amounts, claim/account numbers, and names from your documents. Do not leave stray placeholder brackets behind.
2. **Stick to verifiable facts:** State what happened, what documents you attached, and what specific outcome you are asking for (such as a corrected bill, financial assistance review, debt validation, or written hold).
3. **Do not guess legal conclusions or dates:** If a service date, first bill date, or payment date is unclear, check your records first rather than guessing.
4. **Keep a complete packet:** Save a copy of the filled template, every attachment (itemized bill, EOB, notices), and proof of when and how you sent it.

## Lay-Person Record & Template Checklist

- [ ] Requested and received an **itemized bill** with service dates, codes, units, and line-item charges.
- [ ] Downloaded or saved the matching **Explanation of Benefits (EOB)** from the insurer.
- [ ] Recorded **key dates** (date of service, first statement date, due/appeal deadlines, and any prior payments or calls).
- [ ] Selected the matching template from [[Template-Library]] and checked [[Before-You-Fill-Out-a-Template]].
- [ ] Filled in every bracketed field accurately and removed leftover placeholder brackets.
- [ ] Attached supporting copies (itemized bill, EOB, relevant notices) and kept a full copy for your records.
- [ ] Logged the **date sent, delivery method, and confirmation/tracking info** so follow-ups are easy to track.
