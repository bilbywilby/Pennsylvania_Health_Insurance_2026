# Checking Service Codes and Unusual Line Items on Medical Bills

Itemized bills often list procedure or service codes alongside descriptions and unit counts. You do not need to memorize every medical code to spot red flags and ask clear questions.

## 1. Practical Red Flags to Look For

- **Unclear or repeated lines:** Multiple lines on the same date that look like separate pieces of one combined service or repeat the same test/item without explanation.
- **Wrong visit type or place:** A bill coded as a higher-level visit, specialty service, or separate facility/supply charge that does not match what happened.
- **Mismatch with your EOB:** A charge on the bill that your EOB shows as denied, non-covered, or higher than your listed patient responsibility.

## 2. How to Follow Up

1. **Ask the billing office to explain the code and charge in plain language** and confirm how the total was calculated.
2. **Check your EOB** to see how your insurer classified the code and whether a discount or adjustment applies.
3. **Ask for a corrected itemized statement in writing** if an error or duplicate/unbundled charge is confirmed, and keep copies of both statements.
