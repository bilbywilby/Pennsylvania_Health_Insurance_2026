# Appealing Denials and External Review Under PA Act 146 of 2022

When a health plan denies coverage or limits payment, **Pennsylvania Act 146 of 2022** provides state-level **independent external review** protections alongside internal plan appeal procedures for regulated health coverage.

## 1. Key Steps in an Appeal

- **Review the denial notice and `EOB`:** Check the stated clinical or coverage reason, whether the service was labeled not medically necessary or experimental/investigational, and the exact filing deadline.
- **Complete the plan's internal appeal first:** Follow the insurer's written internal appeal instructions and attach supporting clinical or billing records.
- **Request external review when eligible:** Under **Act 146 of 2022** (for state-regulated plans, with parallel federal external review rules applying to self-funded or ACA-covered plans as applicable), eligible denials can go to an independent review organization for an outside decision.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Track every deadline on your calendar** from the date of the adverse benefit determination or final internal appeal decision.
2. **Submit supporting records in writing** (such as provider notes, test results, and itemized bills) and keep proof of submission.
3. **Contact the PA Insurance Department (`PID`)** if you need consumer guidance on a state-regulated insurance denial or complaint.
