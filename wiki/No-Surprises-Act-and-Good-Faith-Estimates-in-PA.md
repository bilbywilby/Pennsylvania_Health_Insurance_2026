# No Surprises Act Protections and Good Faith Estimates in PA

Federal **No Surprises Act** rules (`45 CFR Part 149`) limit balance billing for covered emergency and out-of-network facility services and give uninsured or self-pay patients advance **Good Faith Estimates (`GFE`)**.

## 1. Key Statutory Protections

- **Emergency & in-network facility balance billing:** Under the **No Surprises Act**, out-of-network emergency care, air ambulance services, and certain out-of-network ancillary services at an in-network hospital or ambulatory surgical center are generally subject to in-network cost-sharing rules and balance-billing bans unless valid statutory notice and consent apply where permitted.
- **Good Faith Estimates (`GFE`) for uninsured/self-pay care:** Uninsured or self-pay consumers scheduling non-emergency care can request or receive a written estimate of expected charges; final bills exceeding a `GFE` by at least **$400** may qualify for the federal patient-provider dispute process.
- **Check your `EOB` and bill together:** Confirm whether your insurer applied in-network cost sharing and counted payments toward your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Compare the provider bill against your `EOB` or `GFE`** and flag any unexpected out-of-network balance bill or excess charge.
2. **Ask the billing office to pause collection action** while you verify `No Surprises Act` or `GFE` applicability in writing.
3. **Escalate unresolved billing or insurer disputes** through your plan appeal process, the PA Insurance Department (`PID`), or federal `No Surprises` help channels, and keep copies of all records.
