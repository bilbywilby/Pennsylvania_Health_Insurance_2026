# Comparing Hospital Financial Assistance and Payment Plans

When facing a large hospital or health-system bill, check whether you qualify for hospital financial assistance (`FAP`) before agreeing to a long-term payment plan.

## 1. Key Differences to Weigh

- **Hospital Financial Assistance (`FAP`):** May reduce or waive eligible hospital charges based on household income, family size, or program rules—lowering the balance owed rather than stretching out the full amount.
- **Payment Plans:** Spread a balance into monthly installments over time, usually without reducing the underlying principal balance unless combined with an adjustment or discount.
- **Billing hold while applying:** Ask the billing office how to place a temporary hold on collection notices while your assistance application is under review.

## 2. Practical Steps to Take

1. **Request the hospital's financial assistance policy and application** along with a list of accepted income/household documents.
2. **Confirm what services are covered** (such as hospital facility bills versus independent physician or lab groups).
3. **Keep copies of your application, proof of submission, and written decisions.**
