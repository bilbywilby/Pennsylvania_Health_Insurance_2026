# Nonprofit Hospital Financial Assistance: IRC Section 501(r) and PA Act 55

Nonprofit hospitals in Pennsylvania are subject to federal **IRC Section 501(r)** (`26 U.S.C. § 501(r)` and `26 CFR § 1.501(r)-6`) financial assistance rules as well as state charity-care expectations under **Pennsylvania Act 55 of 1997** (the Institutions of Purely Public Charity Act).

## 1. Core Rules to Know

- **Written Financial Assistance Policy (`FAP`):** Covered nonprofit hospitals must maintain a written `FAP` explaining eligibility criteria, how to apply, and how amounts charged to eligible patients are limited relative to **Amounts Generally Billed (`AGB`)** to insured patients.
- **120-day and 240-day timelines under `26 CFR § 1.501(r)-6`:** Nonprofit hospitals generally must wait at least **120 days** after the first post-discharge billing statement before taking **Extraordinary Collection Actions (`ECAs`)** (such as lawsuits, property liens, or credit-bureau reporting) and provide at least **240 days** from the first post-discharge statement to apply for financial assistance.
- **State community benefit context (`Act 55 of 1997`):** Pennsylvania's **Act 55 of 1997** sets state standards for institutions of purely public charity, including providing charity care or community benefits.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Request the hospital's written `FAP`, application form, and income/document checklist** before paying a large balance or signing a payment agreement.
2. **Ask for a written hold on collection or `ECA` steps** while you submit a complete financial assistance application.
3. **Save your application, proof of delivery, and written determination** in your health folder.
