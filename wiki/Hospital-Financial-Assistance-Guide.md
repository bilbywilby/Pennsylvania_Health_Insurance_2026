# Hospital Financial Assistance Guide

**Step 1 -- Determine hospital nonprofit status** -- § 501(r) obligations apply to 501(c)(3) hospitals.

**Step 2 -- Request the Financial Assistance Policy (FAP) and application in writing**; they must publish and hold the account during review.

**Step 3 -- Submit complete application** and calendar their response window.

**Step 4 -- Denial? Demand written justification** tied to published FAP criteria and escalate per the dispute ladder.

## What This Means in Real Life (Step-by-Step Example)

Suppose you had an outpatient procedure or ER visit and your household income is modest compared to the bill, or you lost hours/work and cannot afford the lump sum.

1. **Ask the hospital billing department directly for their Financial Assistance Policy (`FAP`) application and plain-language summary**, even if you already received a statement or partial bill.
2. **Submit the application and income/household proof before paying down the balance**, and ask in writing for the account to be placed on hold while the application is under review.
3. **Keep proof of submission** (confirmation number, email receipt, or certified mail/drop-off log) and check whether discounts apply on a sliding scale or full write-off based on your household size and income guidelines.
