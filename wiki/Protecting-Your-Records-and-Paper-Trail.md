# Organizing Your Bills, EOBs, and Paper Trail

When you are dealing with medical bills, insurance claims, or collection letters, a simple folder system saves hours of stress later.

## 1. Keep One Folder Per Bill or Episode of Care

- Group documents by **date of service** and **provider/hospital name** (either a physical folder or a clear digital folder with PDFs/photos).
- Keep the **itemized bill**, **insurer EOB**, **denial or collection letters**, **financial assistance forms**, and **payment receipts** together.

## 2. Keep a Simple Call and Letter Log

- Write down a short line for every contact:
  - **Date and time**
  - **Who you spoke with or sent it to** (name, department, phone/email/address)
  - **Reference number or tracking/confirmation ID**
  - **Summary of what was said or requested**

## 3. Practical Safety Habits

1. **Never rely on memory for deadlines or promises:** Write down due dates, hold confirmations, and promised adjustments right away.
2. **Keep copies of everything before sending:** Always retain a copy of completed forms, letters, and attachments.
