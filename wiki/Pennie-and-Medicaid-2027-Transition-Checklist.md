# Pennie and Coverage Transition Checklist for 2027

Health coverage rules, renewal notices, and marketplace costs can shift heading into 2027. Use this checklist to keep dates and account details organized.

## 1. Key Dates to Put on Your Calendar

- **Pennie Open Enrollment:** Runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**.
- **January 1, 2027 Coverage Deadline:** Select or renew a Pennie plan by **December 15, 2026** for coverage starting **January 1, 2027** (enrollments from December 16 through January 15 start **February 1, 2027**).
- **January 1, 2027 Program Changes:** Watch for renewal notices and reporting rules across Pennie and Pennsylvania Medicaid/Medical Assistance.

## 2. Practical Consumer Checklist

1. **Keep mailing address, phone, email, and household/income info current** on your Pennie or PA DHS/COMPASS account so important notices are not missed.
2. **Check net premiums, deductibles, and network status** for your doctors and prescriptions before picking or renewing a plan.
3. **Save confirmation pages and renewal letters** in a dedicated 2027 coverage folder.
