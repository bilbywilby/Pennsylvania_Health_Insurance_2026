# Pennsylvania Medicaid (MA) and CHIP Basics for 2027

Pennsylvania Medicaid (also called Medical Assistance, or MA) and CHIP help cover eligible children, parents, caregivers, and adults. Keeping your contact info current and watching renewal dates helps protect coverage.

## 1. Key 2027 Changes to Watch

- **Six-month renewals for some adults:** Starting **January 1, 2027**, some adults ages 19–64 will complete eligibility renewals every six months instead of once a year.
- **80-hour monthly activity or exemption rules:** Starting **January 1, 2027**, covered adults ages 19–64 who do not have a dependent child under age 14 and are not exempt must show at least 80 hours per month of qualifying activities (such as work, volunteering/community service, job training, or at least half-time school—or earning at least $580/month) or document an exemption.

## 2. Everyday Steps to Stay Covered

1. **Update your address, phone, and email** in PA DHS COMPASS (`dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS` or `myCOMPASS PA`) and with USPS, and opt into DHS text/email alerts (`dhs.pa.gov/text`).
2. **Open PA DHS mail right away** and respond to renewal packets or information requests before the due date.
3. **Keep copies of notices, submitted forms, and work/exemption records** in one folder.
