# Pennsylvania Health Insurance & Medical Billing Guide (2026–2027)

Plain-language guides, checklists, and Spanish companions (`Guías en Español`) for navigating **Pennie** marketplace coverage, **Pennsylvania Medicaid (MA) / CHIP**, **Explanation of Benefits (`EOB`)** statements, itemized bills, claim appeals, financial assistance, and debt collection protections.

## 1. Key 2027 Dates & Program Updates

- **Pennie 2027 Open Enrollment:** Runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**. Select a plan by **December 15, 2026** for coverage starting **January 1, 2027**, or from **December 16, 2026 through January 15, 2027** for coverage starting **February 1, 2027** (subject to initial payment deadlines).
- **PA Medicaid (MA) Changes Starting January 1, 2027:** Certain adults ages 19–64 face six-month renewal cycles; covered adults ages 19–64 without a dependent child under age 14 or an exemption generally must show at least **80 hours per month** of qualifying activities (work, volunteering/community service, job training, or at least half-time/part-time school—or earn at least **$580/month**) or document an exemption; retroactive coverage windows also narrow starting **January 1, 2027**.
- **Official Portals & Contacts:** Use `pennie.com` / **844-844-8040** for Pennie marketplace coverage, `dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS` / `myCOMPASS PA` / **877-395-8930** for PA DHS Medicaid/CHIP, and the **PA Insurance Department (`PID`)** for state-regulated insurance questions or complaints.
