# Understanding Retroactive Medicaid and Coverage Start Dates

Coverage start dates and retroactive coverage windows affect which medical bills a plan can cover—especially after a gap, job/income change, or new application.

## 1. Key 2027 Rules to Watch

- **Pennie 2027 start dates:** Open Enrollment runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**; plan selections by **December 15, 2026** start **January 1, 2027**, while selections from **December 16, 2026 through January 15, 2027** start **February 1, 2027** (subject to initial payment rules).
- **Shortened PA Medicaid retroactive coverage starting January 1, 2027:** Retroactive coverage windows narrow starting **January 1, 2027** (up to one month prior for Medicaid expansion adults ages 19–64 and up to two months prior for other eligible recipients, compared to up to three months previously).
- **Active date vs. bill date:** Always match each visit's date of service to the plan and dates active on that day.

## 2. How to Protect Yourself

1. **Apply or renew as early as possible** and track your exact effective start date in Pennie or PA DHS (`COMPASS` / `myCOMPASS PA`).
2. **Share updated coverage or retroactive approval notices** with provider billing offices promptly.
3. **Keep copies of applications, approval letters, and bills** organized by date of service.
