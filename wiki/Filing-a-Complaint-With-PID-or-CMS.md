# Filing a Consumer Complaint or Escalation: How to Prepare Your Facts

When routine calls or billing/insurer letters do not resolve a billing or coverage problem, organizing a clear written summary with your records helps you escalate cleanly.

## 1. Gather Your Timeline and Documents First

- **Put key dates in order:** Date of service, date of first bill or denial notice, date of EOB, appeal/response deadlines, and dates of calls or letters.
- **Attach clear copies of key records:** Itemized bill, insurer EOB, denial or collection letters, and copies of what you already sent.

## 2. Choose the Right Path and Template

1. **Match your plan type and issue:** Review [[Before-You-Fill-Out-a-Template]] and [[Template-Library]] to see which template fits your situation.
2. **Use the matching template page:** Such as [[Template-PID-Complaint-Narrative-Fully-Insured]], [[Template-CMS-Complaint-Narrative-Self-Funded-ERISA]], or [[Template-Act-252-NSA-Rescission-Demand]].
3. **Keep a complete copy of everything submitted** along with the date sent and any confirmation number.
