# Good Faith Estimates and Self-Pay Billing: What to Check

If you are uninsured, not using a health plan for a service, or paying on your own, getting an estimate up front and comparing it to your final bill helps catch unexpected charges.

## 1. Before Scheduled Care

- **Ask for a written Good Faith Estimate (`GFE`):** For scheduled care when uninsured or self-paying, ask what expected charges are included for the main service and any related items or tests.
- **Ask about financial assistance or cash/self-pay discounts:** Check whether the hospital offers a Financial Assistance Policy (`FAP`) or self-pay discount rules before care or before paying.

## 2. When the Final Bill Arrives

1. **Request an itemized bill** showing each service, code, unit count, and charge.
2. **Compare the final bill against your estimate** line by line and flag new or much higher charges.
3. **Keep copies of your estimate, itemized bill, and written questions** in one folder.
