# Insurer Dispute Playbook

**Step 1 — Get the denial in writing** with the specific policy provision cited.

**Step 2 — Exhaust internal appeals** (calendar every deadline).

**Step 3 — Fully insured plan? File with the PA Insurance Department** and invoke Act 146 external review.

**Step 4 — Self-funded employer plan (ERISA)?** Federal external review via CMS is the route, not the PID.
