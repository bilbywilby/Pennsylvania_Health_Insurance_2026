# Start Here: Which Situation Are You In?

Answer honestly, then open the matching playbook. When in doubt, choose the earliest stage you can identify — earlier stages have stronger statutory protections.

1. **"The bill arrived and it's more than I was quoted."** → Was anyone on the bill *out-of-network* (ER, hospitalist, anesthesiologist)? → [[Surprise Bill Playbook|Surprise-Bill-Playbook]]
2. **"The bill is months old and a collector is calling."** → [[Medical Debt Defense Playbook|Medical-Debt-Defense-Playbook]]
3. **"I can't pay anything at all."** → Hospital may owe you assistance under IRS § 501(r). → [[Hospital Financial Assistance Guide|Hospital-Financial-Assistance-Guide]]
4. **"My insurer said the claim is denied."** → Internal appeal deadlines are short. → [[Insurer Dispute Playbook|Insurer-Dispute-Playbook]]
5. **"I was served with a lawsuit."** → Do not ignore the deadline to respond (~20 days in PA). → [[Medical Debt Defense Playbook|Medical-Debt-Defense-Playbook]], *Responding to a Lawsuit* section.

**Do this first, for any situation:** keep every bill, EOB, and letter; note every phone call (date, name, what was said); never admit liability verbally; and never pay a penny toward an old debt before reading [[Medical Debt Defense Playbook|Medical-Debt-Defense-Playbook]] — a small payment can restart the statute of limitations (42 Pa.C.S. § 5525(a)).
