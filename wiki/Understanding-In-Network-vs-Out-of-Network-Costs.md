# In-Network vs. Out-of-Network Costs in Plain English

Health plans negotiate rates with **in-network** doctors, clinics, and hospitals. Going **out-of-network** can change how much your plan pays, whether cost sharing counts toward your limits, and whether extra billing rules apply.

## 1. Key Differences Consumers Notice

- **In-network providers:** Agree to contracted rates with your plan, which typically keeps your cost share lower and helps count eligible spending toward your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
- **Out-of-network providers:** May not have contracted rates with your plan, leading to different coverage rules, higher charges, or separate billing questions unless an emergency or network protection applies.

## 2. Practical Steps to Take

1. **Check network status before care** when possible and keep a record of your check.
2. **Read your EOB carefully** to see how your insurer classified the provider and calculated your responsibility.
3. **Question unexpected out-of-network charges** on emergency visits or in-network hospital care and keep written copies of your follow-up.
