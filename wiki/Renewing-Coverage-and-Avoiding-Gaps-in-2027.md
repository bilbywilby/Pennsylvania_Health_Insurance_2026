# Renewing Coverage and Avoiding Gaps in 2027

Missed letters, outdated addresses, or missed renewal dates can cause avoidable gaps in health coverage. Checking your accounts and saving notices early helps prevent surprises.

## 1. Pennie and Medicaid Renewal Basics

- **Pennie Open Enrollment:** Runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**; select or review your plan by **December 15, 2026** for **January 1, 2027** coverage (and pay any required first premium on time).
- **Pennsylvania Medicaid / PA DHS updates starting January 1, 2027:** Some adults ages 19–64 may face six-month renewal cycles and 80-hour monthly work/community engagement or exemption reporting at renewal, alongside shortened retroactive coverage windows.

## 2. Everyday Steps to Prevent Gaps

1. **Keep mailing address, phone, and email up to date** in your Pennie account or PA DHS COMPASS (`dhs.pa.gov/COMPASS` / `myCOMPASS PA`) and with USPS.
2. **Open every renewal notice and packet promptly** and note due dates right away.
3. **Keep copies of confirmations, submitted forms, and renewal dates** in your health folder.
