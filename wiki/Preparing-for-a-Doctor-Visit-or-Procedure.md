# Preparing for a Doctor Visit, Scan, or Procedure: Practical Checklist

Asking a few quick questions before an appointment, imaging scan, or outpatient procedure can prevent surprises with network status, referrals, or separate facility/specialist bills.

## 1. Before Your Appointment

- **Verify network status for both location and provider:** Check whether the clinic, hospital, or imaging center—and the specific doctor or specialist—are in-network with your plan.
- **Check if prior authorization or a referral is needed:** Ask your provider's office and check your plan portal for tests, specialty visits, therapies, or procedures that need prior approval.
- **Ask about separate billing:** For procedures or tests, ask whether separate bills may come from a facility, anesthesiologist, radiologist, or lab.

## 2. At the Visit and Afterward

1. **Confirm your current insurance card and contact info** at check-in.
2. **Keep your visit summary, receipts, and any cost estimate** in your care folder.
3. **Match future bills against your EOB** once claims finish processing.
