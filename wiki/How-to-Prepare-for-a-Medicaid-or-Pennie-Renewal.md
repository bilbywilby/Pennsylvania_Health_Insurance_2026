# Preparing for a Pennie or Medicaid Renewal Without Stress

Gathering your current details ahead of time makes renewing or reviewing coverage on Pennie or through Pennsylvania DHS (`COMPASS` or `myCOMPASS PA`) much smoother.

## 1. Key Dates and Updates to Keep in Mind

- **Pennie Open Enrollment:** Runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**, with **December 15, 2026** as the deadline for **January 1, 2027** coverage (and **February 1, 2027** for selections from December 16 through January 15).
- **Pennsylvania Medicaid / MA (starting January 1, 2027):** Watch for six-month renewal cycles for some adults ages 19–64 and 80-hour monthly qualifying activity or exemption reporting (or earning at least $580/month) for covered adults ages 19–64 without a dependent child under age 14 or an exemption, plus shortened retroactive coverage windows.

## 2. Practical Prep Checklist

1. **Confirm your contact info** (mailing address, phone, email) in your account and with USPS so notices reach you.
2. **Gather household, income, and activity/exemption records** before starting your renewal or plan review.
3. **Save confirmation numbers, submitted forms, and renewal dates** in your health folder.
