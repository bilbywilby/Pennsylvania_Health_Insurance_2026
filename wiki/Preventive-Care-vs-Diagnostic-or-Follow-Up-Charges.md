# Preventive Care vs. Diagnostic or Follow-Up Charges

A routine visit can include preventive services alongside diagnostic tests, follow-up care, or separate lab/facility charges that are billed differently.

## 1. Why Charges Can Differ

- **Preventive care:** Routine checkups, screenings, or immunizations covered under preventive rules when billed as preventive services—though additional tests or treatments during the visit may be billed separately.
- **Diagnostic or follow-up care:** Tests, imaging, procedures, or visits ordered to investigate symptoms, track a condition, or treat an issue may trigger deductibles, copays, or coinsurance.
- **Separate labs or specialists:** Outside labs, pathology, or specialists may bill separately from your primary care office.

## 2. Practical Steps to Check a Bill

1. **Review your `EOB` and itemized bill line by line** to see how each service code was processed.
2. **Ask how each line was coded and why** if a routine visit includes unexpected cost sharing.
3. **Save your `EOB`, visit summary, and itemized statement** together.
