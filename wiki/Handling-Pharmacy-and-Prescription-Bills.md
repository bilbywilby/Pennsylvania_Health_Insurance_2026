# Handling Pharmacy and Prescription Charges Step by Step

Prescription prices at the pharmacy counter can vary depending on whether a drug is on your plan's formulary, whether a preferred pharmacy is used, or whether prior authorization is required.

## 1. Before Leaving the Pharmacy Counter

- **Ask what the charge is based on:** Check whether the price reflects your plan's copay/coinsurance, a deductible not yet met, a non-preferred tier, a missing prior authorization, or an out-of-network pharmacy.
- **Ask if a generic or preferred formulary option exists:** Ask the pharmacist or prescriber whether an equivalent covered option is available if a cash or non-formulary price looks high.
- **Save the itemized pharmacy receipt:** Keep the receipt showing the drug name, fill date, quantity, prescription/claim details, and amount paid.

## 2. If You Paid Out of Pocket or Got a Denial

1. **Check your insurer portal or EOB/pharmacy claim record** to see how the claim processed.
2. **Ask your prescriber's office** if prior authorization or clinical notes are needed for ongoing coverage.
3. **Keep receipts and reference numbers** if you submit a reimbursement request or appeal.
