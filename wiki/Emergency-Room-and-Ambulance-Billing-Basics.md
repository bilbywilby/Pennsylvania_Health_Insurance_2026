# Emergency Room and Ambulance Bills: Practical Steps

Emergency care often involves multiple bills—such as the emergency department, doctors, labs, imaging, or an ambulance—so checking each statement against your insurer's EOB is essential.

## 1. What to Look For on Emergency and Transport Bills

- **Emergency room and hospital/specialist bills:** Check how your insurer processed emergency services and whether in-network cost-sharing rules or surprise-billing protections apply.
- **Air vs. ground ambulance:** Federal No Surprises Act rules cover air ambulances, while ground ambulances follow different plan and state/local billing rules—so check your EOB and insurer rules for each transport bill separately.

## 2. How to Review and Follow Up

1. **Group all bills from the same emergency date** together with your insurer's EOBs.
2. **Check whether any bill asks for more than your EOB** or lists unclear extra charges.
3. **Ask questions or dispute in writing** and save copies of every notice and reference number.
