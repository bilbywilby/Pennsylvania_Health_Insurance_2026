# Facility Charges vs. Doctor Charges on Hospital and Clinic Bills

When you visit a hospital outpatient department or a health-system-owned clinic, you may see separate charges—or even two separate bills—for the **facility** and the **provider** who treated you.

## 1. Why Separate Charges Appear

- **Facility / clinic charge:** Covers the building, nursing/support staff, equipment, or clinic operations billed by the facility or health system.
- **Professional / clinician charge:** Covers the doctor, nurse practitioner, physician assistant, or specialist evaluation or procedure.

## 2. How to Review These Charges

1. **Ask for an itemized bill** showing each line item, date of service, code, units, and charge rather than a single combined balance.
2. **Compare against your EOB** to see how your insurer processed each claim, applied network discounts, and calculated your patient responsibility.
3. **Ask questions in writing** if a facility fee or line item is unclear or higher than expected, and keep copies of everything.
