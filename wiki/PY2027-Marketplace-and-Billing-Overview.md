# PY2027 Pennsylvania Marketplace and Medical Billing Overview

## Key 2027 Coverage Dates (Pennie)

- **Open Enrollment Window:** November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027.
- **January 1, 2027 Coverage Deadline:** Enroll or select a plan by December 15, 2026.
- **Renewal Review:** Check renewal notices for net 2027 premiums after any applicable tax credits and compare plans in your county on Pennie.

## PY2027 Rate Review and Marketplace Context

- **PID Rate Filings:** Pennsylvania Insurance Department (PID) approved weighted average rate increases of **15.97%** for the individual market (down from 17.14% requested) and **10.28%** for the small-group market (down from 11.49% requested), denying roughly **$95 million** (`$94.7M`) in requested increases.
- **Reinsurance (PA-Re):** Pennsylvania's reinsurance program lowers 2027 benchmark premiums by an estimated **3.0%** according to PID.
- **Subsidy and Enrollment Shifts:** Expiration of enhanced federal premium tax credits and rising medical/drug claim costs have increased unsubsidized premiums and contributed to enrollment shifts from roughly 497,000 in 2025 toward ~417,000 as of September 2026.

## Medical Bill and No Surprises Act Audit Checklist

1. **Request an itemized bill** with CPT/HCPCS codes, modifier codes, units, and billed amounts alongside the insurer Explanation of Benefits (EOB).
2. **Check out-of-network and emergency billing** under federal No Surprises Act rules (`45 CFR Part 149`) and verify in-network cost-sharing application.
3. **Benchmark pricing and coding** against Medicare Physician Fee Schedule (MPFS) / locality multipliers and NCCI bundling rules before disputing or negotiating.
4. **Verify statutory citations and deadlines** independently prior to regulatory complaints, appeals, or legal filings.
