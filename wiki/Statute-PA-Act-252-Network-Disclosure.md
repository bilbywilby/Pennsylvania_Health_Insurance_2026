# PA Act 252 of 2023 -- Network Disclosure

**Plain-language summary:** Pennsylvania's Act 252 (2023) requires insurers to disclose more about their provider networks and -- critically for surprise bills -- strengthens rules about assigning you to providers without your consent. If you were treated by a provider you never knowingly chose, and your insurer or hospital network landed you there, Act 252's disclosure obligations support your argument that you shouldn't owe the balance.

**What to do:** Pair an Act 252 disclosure argument with the federal NSA claim in the rescission-demand template. The two authorities reinforce each other: NSA bars the bill; Act 252 shows the disclosure failure that let it happen.

**Key limits to know:**
- It's a disclosure/network-transparency statute -- remedies typically run through the PA Insurance Department complaint process rather than direct court action.
- It complements, but does not replace, the NSA.

## In Plain English: How This Protects You

Network transparency and disclosure rules help you spot missing or unclear network information when a provider or plan bills you unexpectedly. Keep copies of your plan network lookup, EOB, and provider statements when questioning an unexpected charge.
