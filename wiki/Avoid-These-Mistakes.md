# Avoid These Mistakes

From *THREE THINGS THAT CAN HARM YOUR CASE* and the Critical Consumer Rules:

1. **Making a small "good faith" payment** on an old debt — this can restart the statute of limitations (§ 5525(a)) and revive a dying claim.
2. **Ignoring a lawsuit** — a default judgment converts a disputed claim into an enforceable judgment with asset-seizure power.
3. **Admitting the debt verbally** — collector call recordings may later be used against you; route all substantive communication in writing.
4. **Losing documents** — your EOB and itemized bill are the backbone of every dispute; digitize them on day one.
5. **Assuming the hospital checked you for charity care** — nonprofits often must screen for § 501(r) eligibility *before* extraordinary collection actions, but verification is on you.
