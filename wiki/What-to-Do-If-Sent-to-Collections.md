# What to Do If a Medical Bill Is Sent to Collections

Getting a notice from a collection agency can be stressful, but taking a calm, organized approach helps you verify the claim and avoid hasty decisions.

## 1. First Steps When a Collector Contacts You

- **Do not panic or rush into a payment on the spot:** Gather your existing bills, insurer EOBs, and payment/assistance records first.
- **Ask for written validation:** Request written details showing the collector's name, original creditor/provider, account/claim details, and an itemized breakdown of the balance claimed.
- **Check whether financial assistance or an insurance dispute was still pending:** If you applied for hospital financial assistance (`FAP`) or were waiting on an insurer or surprise-billing review, gather proof of those steps.

## 2. Keeping Clean Records

1. **Save every letter and envelope:** Keep collection notices, payment demands, and dates received in one folder.
2. **Note dates carefully:** Track the date of service, date of first bill, any prior payments, and when collection contact began.
3. **Avoid treating disputed or unclear demands casually:** Keep written records of any dispute, request, or follow-up.
