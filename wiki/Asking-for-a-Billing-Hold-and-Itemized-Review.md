# Asking for a Billing Hold While You Review a Bill or Apply for Aid

When a bill is confusing, under insurance review, or tied to a financial assistance application, asking for a billing hold and itemized review gives you time to verify the numbers before paying or facing escalation.

## 1. When to Ask for a Hold

- You requested an **itemized bill** and are comparing it against your insurer's **EOB**.
- You are waiting on an **insurance claim review, surprise-bill check, or appeal**.
- You are gathering documents to apply for **Hospital Financial Assistance (`FAP`)**.

## 2. Practical Steps to Take

1. **Ask clearly for an itemized statement and a temporary hold** on collection/escalation while your review or financial assistance application is in progress.
2. **Note the date, person/department, and reference number** every time you call, use a portal message, or send a letter.
3. **Follow up in writing** and keep copies of what you sent and received.
