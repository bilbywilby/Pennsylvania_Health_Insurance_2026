# How to Check Doctor, Clinic, and Hospital Network Status

Whether a provider is **in-network** or **out-of-network** can change what your plan pays and how much you owe out of pocket. Checking network status ahead of time—and saving proof—helps prevent unexpected bills.

## 1. Check Before Your Appointment When Possible

- **Look up the provider directly through your insurer:** Use your health plan's official directory or call member services with the provider's full name, specialty, clinic address, and **NPI** (provider ID number) if listed.
- **Check both the facility and the individual provider:** A hospital can be in-network while an independent clinic, lab, or specialist group is not—and vice versa.
- **Save proof of the check:** Take a screenshot or write down the date, directory details, or representative reference number in case a billing dispute comes up later.

## 2. What to Do If a Bill Looks Out-of-Network

1. **Compare your EOB and itemized bill** to see why the insurer treated the claim as out-of-network.
2. **Check emergency and facility rules:** Emergency visits and certain services at in-network hospitals have special cost-sharing protections.
3. **Ask for a written review or dispute** if you relied on inaccurate network info or believe in-network rules were missed.
