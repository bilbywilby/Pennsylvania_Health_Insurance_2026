# Payment Plans vs. Hospital Financial Assistance: Practical Tradeoffs

When a medical bill is larger than you can pay at once, signing up for the first payment plan offered is not always the best first move—especially at a hospital with a Financial Assistance Policy (`FAP`).

## 1. Check Financial Assistance First for Hospital Bills

- **Ask about financial assistance (`FAP` or charity care) before locking in payments:** Depending on household income, family size, and hospital policy, eligible patients may get a full write-off or sliding-scale discount.
- **Request a written hold during application review:** Ask the hospital billing office to pause collection activity while your application and supporting documents are reviewed.

## 2. Comparing Common Options

- **Hospital Financial Assistance (`FAP`):** Can reduce or eliminate eligible hospital balances based on income/need rules; always ask for the written application and checklist first.
- **Interest-free or low-burden provider payment plan:** Only commit to a monthly amount you can realistically sustain after verifying that the bill itself is accurate and aligned with your EOB.
- **High-interest credit cards or loans:** Moving a medical bill onto a credit card generally converts a medical balance into standard consumer credit debt with card interest rates.
