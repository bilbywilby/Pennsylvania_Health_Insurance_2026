# Talking to Billers, Insurers, and Financial Counselors: What to Ask and Log

Phone calls with billing offices or insurance representatives go smoother when you have your documents open and write down concrete details instead of relying on memory.

## 1. Before You Dial

- Have your **itemized bill**, **EOB**, **member/policy ID**, **claim or account number**, and **dates of service** in front of you.
- Have a pen and paper (or note file) ready to record the **date, time, representative name or ID, call reference number, and answer given**.

## 2. Key Questions to Ask

- **When calling the health insurer:**
  - "How was claim `[claim number]` processed for service date `[date]`—in-network or out-of-network?"
  - "What amount does the EOB list as my patient responsibility, and why?"
  - "Does any surprise billing protection or appeal deadline apply to this claim?"
- **When calling the provider or hospital billing office:**
  - "Can you send an itemized statement with every service code, unit count, and charge?"
  - "Why does the balance on this bill differ from my EOB?"
  - "How do I apply for Financial Assistance (`FAP`), and can you place a temporary hold on collection while I apply?"

## 3. After the Call

1. **Write down the exact summary:** Record who you spoke with, the reference number, any promised hold or adjustment, and any deadline mentioned.
2. **Follow up in writing when needed:** Confirm important requests—such as an itemized bill, financial assistance application, or dispute—in writing and keep copies.
