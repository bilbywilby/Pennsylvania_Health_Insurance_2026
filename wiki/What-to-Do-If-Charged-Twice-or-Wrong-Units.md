# Spotting Duplicate Charges, Wrong Units, or Wrong Dates on a Bill

Billing mistakes sometimes show up as the same service billed twice, unit counts that seem much higher than expected, or dates/items that do not match your visit.

## 1. Common Clues to Look For

- **Duplicate lines:** Two similar or identical charges on the same date of service without a clear reason.
- **High unit counts:** A quantity/unit column that multiplies a routine supply, test, or service unexpectedly.
- **Mismatched dates or visits:** Services listed on a day you were not seen or from an episode already settled.

## 2. How to Check and Follow Up

1. **Pull your itemized bill and EOB side by side** and highlight any line with unusual units, repeats, or unclear codes.
2. **Ask the billing office for a line-by-line explanation or corrected statement** before paying the disputed portion.
3. **Save both the original bill and any revised statement** so you have a clear record of what changed.
