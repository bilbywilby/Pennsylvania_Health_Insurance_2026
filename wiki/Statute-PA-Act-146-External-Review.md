# PA Act 146 -- External Review After Appeal Denial

**Plain-language summary:** When your **fully insured** Pennsylvania plan denies a claim (or you exhaust internal appeals and are still denied), Act 146 gives you the right to external review -- an independent reviewer, not your insurer, decides the dispute. This is the strongest lever a fully insured patient has after an appeal fails.

**What to do:** Follow the escalation ladder in the [[Insurer Dispute Playbook|Insurer-Dispute-Playbook]]: written denial -> internal appeal -> PID complaint -> Act 146 external review. Use the [[Template: PID Complaint Narrative (Fully Insured)|Template-PID-Complaint-Narrative-Fully-Insured]] as the vehicle.

**Key limits to know:**
- Fully insured plans only -- self-funded ERISA plans go through the federal external-review route instead (see the CMS template).
- Deadlines matter: external-review requests generally must be filed within a statutory window after final internal denial.

**Citation block:**
- Act of Jul. 5, 2012, P.L. 965, No. 76 (Act 76 of 2012), amended by later acts -- toolkit cites this framework as "Act 146"; verify the current external-review act number via the Pennsylvania Insurance Department's website.

## In Plain English: How This Protects You

When a health insurer denies coverage or medical necessity on an appealable claim, external review gives an independent reviewer a look outside the insurer's own internal decision. Save every denial notice, deadline date, and clinical/billing record in one folder.
