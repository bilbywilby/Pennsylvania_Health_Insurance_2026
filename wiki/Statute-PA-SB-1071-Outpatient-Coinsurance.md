# PA SB 1071 -- Outpatient Coinsurance Limits

**Plain-language summary:** This Pennsylvania legislation addresses how much insurers can require patients to pay for outpatient services -- capping outpatient coinsurance so that a hospital outpatient procedure doesn't cost dramatically more than the same service in a freestanding setting. If your outpatient bill includes a coinsurance amount that looks wildly out of proportion, this statute is a benchmark argument for negotiation or a PID complaint.

**What to do:** If an outpatient coinsurance amount seems excessive, cite the disproportionate-cost principle in your dispute letter, and file with the PID using the [[Template: PID Complaint Narrative (Fully Insured)|Template-PID-Complaint-Narrative-Fully-Insured]] if you're fully insured.

**Key limits to know:**
- Applies to insurers' *design* of cost sharing, not directly to providers' billing.
