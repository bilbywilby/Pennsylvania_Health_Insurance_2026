# What to Do When a Claim or Coverage Request Is Denied

A denial notice from your insurer or pharmacy/care plan is not always the final word. Reading why a request was denied and acting before the deadline are the first steps.

## 1. Read the Denial Notice Carefully

- **Find the exact reason and date:** Look for the denial code or plain-language reason (such as prior authorization needed, not medically necessary, out-of-network, or coding/filing issue) and the **appeal deadline**.
- **Compare with your EOB and visit records:** Make sure the service date, provider, and service description match what actually happened.

## 2. Practical Steps to Take

1. **Calendar the deadline right away:** Appeal windows can be strict, so note the exact due date on the notice immediately.
2. **Call the insurer and ask the provider's office for help:** Ask the insurer what specific missing info or clinical notes would support a review, and ask the doctor's office if they can submit supporting records or corrected coding.
3. **Send your appeal or complaint in writing:** Keep copies of the denial letter, supporting records, submission date, and any confirmation or tracking number.
