# Medical Debt Collection Rules: FDCPA and Pennsylvania Protections

Third-party debt collectors must follow the federal **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (`FDCPA`)**, `15 U.S.C. § 1692` et seq., while Pennsylvania law adds strong consumer protections against wage garnishment and marital property seizure.

## 1. Key Protections for Consumers

- **No abusive, deceptive, or unfair collector conduct (`FDCPA`):** Collectors cannot misrepresent the amount owed, threaten actions they cannot legally take, or contact you at prohibited times or places without proper notice.
- **Debt validation rights:** Under the `FDCPA`, consumers can request written verification of a debt and dispute inaccurate or unsubstantiated balances.
- **Pennsylvania wage garnishment & property rules:** Pennsylvania law generally bars wage garnishment for ordinary consumer and medical debts (subject to narrow statutory exceptions such as child support, taxes, or certain court/government obligations), and jointly held marital property has distinct protections when only one spouse owes a debt.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Verify the debt and ask for an itemized statement** before paying a collection agency or agreeing to settle.
2. **Keep a log of every call, letter, and collection notice** and watch court summonses or deadlines closely if a lawsuit is ever filed.
3. **Check whether financial assistance (`501(r)` / `Act 55`) or an insurance error** applies to the underlying bill.
