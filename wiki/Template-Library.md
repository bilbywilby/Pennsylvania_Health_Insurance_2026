# Template Library

Copy-paste letters and notices. Each template page will include a **fill-these-fields table** and the statutory hook it invokes. Coming from the main repository:

- Formal Debt Validation & Statutory Dispute Notice
- Standardized Act 252 & No Surprises Act Notice of Statutory Dispute and Demand for Complete Rescission
- Provider Escalation & Administrative Hold Demand (Post-Rejection)
- IRS § 501(r) Financial Assistance Demand Letter
- PID Complaint Narrative (fully insured) / CMS Complaint Narrative (self-funded ERISA)
- Complete Dispute Package Checklist & Exhibits

**Rule of thumb:** never mail a dispute without delivery confirmation, and keep a stamped copy of everything sent.
