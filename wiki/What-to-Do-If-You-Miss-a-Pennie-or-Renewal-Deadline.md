# What to Do Around a Pennie or Renewal Deadline

Missing an enrollment cutoff, payment due date, or renewal packet can delay coverage or cause a gap. Checking your account status and acting quickly helps you see what options apply.

## 1. Key 2027 Dates to Check

- **Pennie Open Enrollment:** Runs **November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027**; selecting a plan by **December 15, 2026** starts coverage **January 1, 2027**, while selections from **December 16, 2026 through January 15, 2027** start **February 1, 2027**.
- **Pennsylvania Medicaid / PA DHS:** Watch your renewal date in COMPASS or `myCOMPASS PA` and respond to notices before the due date.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Log into your Pennie or COMPASS/myCOMPASS PA account** right away to check your status, messages, and any open tasks or grace/deadline notices.
2. **Check whether a Special Enrollment Period (`SEP`) or re-application option applies** if a window has closed or a life/coverage change occurred.
3. **Save confirmation screens, letters, and payment receipts** in your coverage folder.
