# What to Do When One Visit Generates Multiple Bills

A single doctor appointment, lab draw, imaging scan, or hospital visit can result in separate statements from the facility, a physician group, a lab, or an imaging center.

## 1. Why Multiple Statements Happen

- **Separate billing entities:** Even inside one health system or hospital, doctors, labs, radiologists, anesthesiologists, or clinics may bill under separate account numbers or tax IDs.
- **Timing differences:** Claims and statements for the same visit often arrive days or weeks apart.

## 2. How to Track and Check Them

1. **Group statements by date of service** in one folder and match each one to its corresponding insurer **EOB**.
2. **Check whether any bill duplicates a charge** or asks for more than your EOB lists as patient responsibility.
3. **Ask for an itemized bill** on any statement where charges, codes, or balances are unclear.
