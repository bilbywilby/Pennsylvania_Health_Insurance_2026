# How to Appeal a Claim Denial or Question a High Bill

If your insurer denies a claim or leaves you with an unexpected balance, review your Explanation of Benefits (`EOB`), itemized bill, and appeal deadline right away.

## 1. Key Steps Before You File

- **Check the deadline on your notice:** Insurers and hospitals set strict appeal or dispute windows on denial letters and billing statements.
- **Compare EOB vs. itemized bill:** Confirm whether the claim was denied, applied to a deductible, treated as out-of-network, or billed for an unclear code or unit count.
- **Gather supporting records:** Collect the denial notice, `EOB`, itemized statement, clinical/referral notes, and dates/names of anyone you spoke with.

## 2. Practical Next Steps

1. **Ask the insurer for the exact reason and appeal instructions** in writing, including internal appeal steps and external review options.
2. **Ask the provider billing office to pause collections or review coding/billing errors** while you verify the claim or request an itemized statement.
3. **Save every letter, submission receipt, and call log** in your coverage folder.
