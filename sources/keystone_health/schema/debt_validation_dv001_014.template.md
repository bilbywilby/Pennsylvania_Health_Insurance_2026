Asserted Claims vs. Verified Primary Authorities

* Template-asserted claims: Clauses inside the letter assert disputed debt status, request proof of authority/itemization, and — when enabled — assert time-barred status, Financial Assistance Policy (FAP) pending/hold status, or communication limits. These are party assertions in the notice, not adjudicated findings.
* Verified primary authorities in scope:
  * 15 U.S.C. §§ 1692g, 1692c(c), 1692e, 1692k: Written validation notice, 30-day dispute/verification rules, communication restrictions, deceptive/time-barred collection prohibitions, and civil liability remedies.
  * 73 P.S. § 2270.4 & 73 P.S. § 201-9.2: Pennsylvania FCEUA coverage (including first-party creditors) and UTPCPL private remedies.
  * 42 Pa.C.S. § 5525(a): Pennsylvania four-year contract/invoice limitations period (avoiding revival via partial payments or unconditional acknowledgments).
  * 26 U.S.C. § 501(r): Hospital facility FAP and Extraordinary Collection Action (ECA) rules.
  * 45 C.F.R. Part 149: Federal No Surprises Act out-of-network emergency/ancillary protections.
* Unverified / external citations: Any statute or bulletin reference not independently confirmed against primary text remains flagged with verification_status "unmatched" rather than asserted as verified. (Neither Act 252 nor SB 1071 is referenced anywhere in this template.)
